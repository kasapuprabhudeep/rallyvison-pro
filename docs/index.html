🏸 RallyVision Pro - Privacy & Security Policy

Effective Date: November 2026 | Application Version: 2.4.0

Core Family & Household Guarantee:

RallyVision Pro is built from the ground up to protect everyday households, families, students, and club badminton players. All match videos, speed metrics, and player analytics are 100% private by default. We never sell, rent, or leak your videos to third-party advertisers.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Details: Name and email address used for secure multi-tenant authentication and profile synchronization.
  • Match Video Files: Videos uploaded or recorded via the device camera specifically for badminton court kinematics, shot classification, and rally detection.
  • Sports Telemetry Data: Kinematic coordinates, player footwork speed, rally durations, and smash velocity calculated locally by the computer vision engine.

2. Camera & Microphone Permissions

  • Camera Access: Used strictly when the user activates the in-app viewfinder to record a live badminton match. No background filming or hidden recording occurs.
  • Microphone Access: Used solely to capture racket string impacts for acoustic smash detection if enabled by the user.

3. Multi-Tenant Isolation & Encryption Standards

Every user account operates within an isolated tenant container. Database keys, session tokens (JWT with SHA-256), and video storage are strictly segregated. Inter-account tampering or unauthorized cross-user access is blocked at the core database level.

4. Children & Household Privacy (COPPA & Families Policy)

RallyVision Pro is family-friendly and safe for householders of all ages. We do not display targeted ads, do not perform behavioral tracking on children, and do not share identifiable information with external data brokers.

5. Data Retention & Automatic Purge

Users maintain complete ownership over their files. Through the in-app Privacy Hub, users can:

  • Set an automatic video auto-purge timer (e.g., 30, 60, or 90 days).
  • Export complete telemetry and match reports in JSON and PDF format.
  • Perform an irreversible, one-click Full Account & Media Purge that immediately wipes all user videos, database rows, and credentials from the server disk.

6. Sharing & Public Links

Matches remain strictly private unless the user explicitly generates a secure, cryptographically random 32-character share key. Public access can be instantly revoked at any moment by unticking the share box in the match panel.
